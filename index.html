Our STUDIO

Cosmic Studios is an independent film finance and production company specializing in fantasy and science-fiction cinema. The studio acquires and develops proven genre IP — stories with established audiences and franchise potential — and brings them to the screen through a disciplined, partnership-driven production model.

Founded on the principle that the best stories become enduring worlds, Cosmic Studios pairs ambitious, audience-first filmmaking with a transparent investment structure built to protect and reward those who help bring these worlds to life.

02

OUR MISSION

Building Blockbuster Films and Franchises in the, action, spy, science fiction and horror genres is our primary focus. By adopting an agile and collaborative business model, we aim to leverage external resources, partnerships, and cutting-edge technologies to streamline our operations and distribution across all platforms.

Our ability to scale projects on the backs of the most successful actors, producers, directors, and writers in the industry sets us apart from other companies and gives us a competitive advantage

OUR SERVICES

  • 01

    Production loan/Investment

    Investment funds are allocated to cover production costs and are secured by pre-sale agreements, in which distributors commit in advance to purchase the completed film—providing a clear path to revenue and mitigating risk

  • 02

    Media loan/Investment

    Strategic collaborations with established producers and talent to develop, finance, and deliver projects with strong creative vision and proven commercial appeal.

  • 03

    First out financing investment

    Capital deployed toward production costs is secured by unsold international territory estimates, independently valued by a reputable foreign sales agent—offering a data-backed basis for risk assessment and potential return.

  • 04

    Finishing funds

    Financing provided when a project has fully utilized its existing resources and requires additional capital to complete production, post-production, or meet delivery requirements—ensuring the project reaches the finish line and becomes market-ready.

  • 05

    Library loans/Investment

    A loan structured to finance the acquisition of a film or television library, or to leverage existing owned content as collateral to fund the acquisition, development, or production of new film and television projects—unlocking asset value to drive portfolio growth.

  • 06

    Tax credit loan/Investment

    A loan secured by state and/or international tax incentives, offering a low-risk financing structure backed by government-issued rebates or credits

  • 07

    Ultimate loan

    Post-theatrical financing provided based on the film’s projected net revenues across all distribution channels, offering investors a revenue-backed opportunity once the film has launched in theaters.

  • 08

    Bridge loan

    A short-term bridge loan designed to meet a producer’s immediate production funding needs—often tied to talent commitments—until long-term financing, such as a bank loan, is secured.

  • 09

    P&A financing

    A short-term bridge loan designed to meet a producer’s immediate production funding needs—often tied to talent commitments—until long-term financing, such as a bank loan, is secured.
